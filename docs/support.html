Cozy Shopkeeper Support

Questions, bugs or ideas? Email SUPPORT_EMAIL. In the app, Settings → Report an issue prepares a report you can send.

Common questions

Does the game need internet? No. It plays fully offline.

Where is my progress saved? On your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes your shop.

A customer refused my price. Lower it with the − button. They give you one more try before leaving. "Use fair price" sets the recommended price.

I'm out of stock and out of coins. Finish the day; a kind neighbor helps you restock.

How do I unlock Flower Lane? Reach reputation level 3 and save 1,200 coins, then open the Map.

Can I start over? Settings → Reset all progress.
