Cozy Shopkeeper Privacy Policy

Effective October 22, 2026 · Applies to the Cozy Shopkeeper app for iPhone

In short: Cozy Shopkeeper collects no personal data. Your game is saved only on your device, and nothing is sent to us or to anyone else.

Information we collect

None. The game has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics service and no server. We do not collect names, email addresses, device identifiers, location, contacts, photos, usage data or diagnostics.

Information stored on your device

Your shop progress (coins, stock, prices, upgrades, decorations, customers met, goals) and your settings (volume, haptics, accessibility options) are saved in the app's private storage on your iPhone. This data never leaves your device through the app. It is removed when you delete the app or choose Settings → Reset all progress. If you use iCloud or iTunes device backups, Apple may include this data in your backups under Apple's own terms.

Tracking

The app does not track you across apps or websites and contains no third-party code that does.

Purchases

This version offers no in-app purchases. If purchases are added in the future, Apple will process them through the App Store; we will never see your payment details or Apple ID.

Reporting a problem

The in-app Report an issue button prepares a short text report (app version, iOS version, device model and basic game progress, with no personal data) and opens the iOS share sheet. Nothing is sent unless you choose an app, such as Mail, and send it yourself. Anything you send is used only to help with your issue.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. You control all game data on your device and can erase it at any time as described above.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
